Serbian Exchange Rates

Daily RSD exchange rates: official NBS list by default, commercial bank lists optional

What it does

  • Fetches the daily exchange-rate list with a scheduled action. Default source: the official National Bank of Serbia list. Alternative sources: Alta Banka (commercial spreads scraped from the bank's website) or any custom endpoint speaking the module's JSON contract.
  • Stores all five published rates per currency and day: buy / middle / sell for wire transfers (devize) and buy / sell for cash (efektiva), including the quote parity (e.g. JPY is quoted per 100 units) and the source of each row.
  • Writes the middle rate into the standard res.currency.rate, so multicurrency accounting, invoicing and reporting use it automatically. Designed for companies with RSD as base currency.
  • Keeps full history: one record per currency per day, browsable under Accounting → Configuration → Serbian Exchange Rates, with a "Fetch rates now" button for manual refresh.
  • Historical backfill of past rates from the official NBS list.

Configuration (system parameters)

  • No configuration required: install, activate the currencies you need (EUR, USD, CHF, …), done — NBS is the default source.
  • rs_fx.rate_source = nbs | alta | custom — choose the daily source.
  • rs_fx.custom_url — endpoint URL when the source is custom.
  • rs_fx.update_currency_rates = 0 — keep the rates in their own table without touching res.currency.rate.
  • rs_fx.ecb_url — redirect the stock ECB provider of Automatic Currency Rates to a self-hosted ECB-format feed.

External services

This module retrieves publicly published exchange rates over HTTPS from a public mirror of the National Bank of Serbia rate list (kurs.resenje.org) and, when selected as source, from altabanka.rs (Alta Banka a.d. Beograd) or a custom endpoint you configure. No customer data is collected or transmitted — requests contain nothing but the date of the requested rate list.
